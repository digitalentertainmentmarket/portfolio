Every Box. Every Tier.

What comes with every rotation.

Regardless of which tier you choose, every Serenity Soule rotation ships with everything you need from the moment it arrives. Nothing extra to source. Nothing left to figure out.

Your first rotation also includes a welcome kit with everything you need to care for your piece through the full 90-day cycle.

01
Your Pre-Customized Piece

Finished to your style profile. Lace tinted, knots bleached, styled, and ready to wear straight from the box.

02
Rotation Certification Card

Every piece ships with a card confirming its rotation status. First wearers receive a First Rotation card. Refurbished pieces receive a Professionally Refurbished card.

03
Your Soule Journey Card

A quarterly keepsake numbered and dated to mark where you are in your Serenity Soule journey. Every quarter a new one arrives. They are worth keeping.

04
Prepaid Return Label

Already in the box. When your 90-day rotation ends, returning your piece takes less than five minutes.

05
Welcome Kit (First Rotation Only)

Wig grip band, satin lined sleep cap, and a tier-specific care card with everything you need to maintain your piece through the full cycle.

Optional Add-On

First Dibs.

When new pieces drop into the Serenity Soule rotation pool, they get assigned to members based on profile fit. First Dibs is an optional add-on that moves you to the front of that line. If a new style arrives and it matches your profile, you get it first before it goes anywhere else.

First Dibs is available as an add-on for any active membership tier. Founding Ciphers receive one free First Dibs per year as part of their perks starting from Year One.

Good to Know

The details that matter.

$50

Refundable Deposit

Every Serenity Soule membership begins with a $50 fully refundable deposit. This is held during your active membership and returned in full when you leave in good standing with your piece returned and your account clear.

△

Damage Policy

Normal wear over a 90-day rotation is expected and covered within the membership. Damage beyond normal wear is assessed on return and a tiered fee applies based on the extent. Full details are in the membership agreement.

▶

Buyout Option

If you fall in love with your rotation piece, keeping it is always an option. Every piece has a dynamic buyout fee calculated based on its condition and tier at the time of the request. Just reach out and Serenity Soule handles the rest.
