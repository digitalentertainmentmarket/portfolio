What Happens When It Comes Back

The refurbishment process.

Every piece that comes back to Serenity Soule goes through a full restoration process before it ever goes back out. Nothing gets skipped and nothing gets rushed.

I

Inspection

Every returning piece is inspected against Serenity Soule's quality standards before anything else happens. Condition is assessed and documented.

II

Deep Cleanse

The piece is professionally washed, conditioned, and treated to restore the hair to its best possible condition before restyling begins.

III

Restore and Restyle

Knots re-bleached if needed. Lace refreshed. The piece is restyled and finished to the same standard it left Serenity Soule the first time.

IV

Ready Again

Once it passes final inspection it is ready for its next rotation. Serenity Soule does not send anything out that is not fully up to standard.

Common Questions

Good questions deserve real answers.

What happens if my piece is damaged during my rotation?

Serenity Soule has a clear damage policy. Normal wear is expected and covered within the membership. Damage beyond normal wear is assessed on return and a tiered damage fee applies depending on the extent. Full details are in your membership agreement.

Can I buy out my piece at the end of a rotation?

Yes. Every piece has a dynamic buyout option. The buyout fee is calculated based on the piece's condition and the tier it belongs to. If you fall in love with your rotation piece, keeping it is always an option.

What is First Dibs?

First Dibs is an optional add-on that gives you early access to new Serenity Soule pieces before they are assigned to the general rotation pool. If a new style drops and you want it, First Dibs gets you there first.

What is the $50 deposit for?

Every Serenity Soule membership requires a $50 refundable deposit at sign-up. This is held during your membership and refunded in full when you leave in good standing, meaning your piece has been returned and your account is clear.

How long does it take to receive my first piece?

Because every piece is finished and prepared specifically around your profile, there is a preparation window after your profile is complete. Serenity Soule will communicate your expected delivery timeline clearly after your membership is confirmed.
