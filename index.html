The Member Journey

How Serenity Soule works.

Four steps is all it takes. Serenity Soule handles everything in between.

01

Join and Profile

Complete your personal style profile. Share your cap size, texture preferences, length preference and style category so Serenity Soule can prepare something made specifically for you.

02

We Customize

Serenity Soule selects, finishes, and prepares your piece based on your style profile. Lace tinted, knots bleached, styled and inspected before it ever gets near a box.

03

Receive and Wear

Your piece arrives ready to wear straight from the box. No stylist appointment. No extra steps. No guesswork. Open the box, put it on, and go.

04

Rotate and Restore

At the end of your 90-day rotation, return your piece using the prepaid label included in your box. Serenity Soule refurbishes it and sends your next rotation, ready to go again.
