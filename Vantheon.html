Our approach, not an industry buzzword

Risk-Based Habit Intelligence

Annual security-awareness training tells you what people were taught. RBHI tells you what they actually do — and turns it into a score you can act on before an incident happens.

  • Tracks real behavior: phishing-simulation responses, password reuse, MFA adoption, and device hygiene
  • Rolls up into a single team risk score, updated weekly, not once a year
  • Flags the specific people and habits driving the score, with a plain-language fix for each one
